# ECLECTIC RESONANCE HOWLING

Scattered memories. The painful yearning of a long gone past. An inherent disturbance in one’s sanity. Surely, we’ve all been at a point in our life when we were helpless, without any idea on how to be free of the shackles holding our soul down… without hope. This album is an elegiac cry to those who feel this way, and for whom the only way to heal is to resonate with the sound of likeminded individuals.

<WrappableParagraph title="Presentation">
HOWLING is an original album compilation focused on the thematic feeling of “hope within dread”. For lack of better words, the general purpose of the album is about giving the listener a feeling of longing, melancholy, nostalgia and yearning. Curating for this album will prove itself to be quite a challenge, and it might be hard for the common producer to properly evaluate the range of such an abstract theme.
</WrappableParagraph>

<WrappableParagraph title="HOWLING Specific Music Rules">
- Your submission must be entirely original in its entirety, and you must hold the rights of your composition. No partial arrangement of any existing product should be present in your submission.
- Ideally, your track needs to be at least 3 minutes long, and cannot exceed 10 minutes.
- We greatly recommend you to check the inspiration playlist to know if your song can fit to the album’s concept.
- The recommended genres list is below, but note that it is possible to do something very different from said list. The key part is to nail the intended soundscape of the album:
    - Artcore
    - Emotional Breakcore & Drum and Bass
    - Intense/Experimental IDM
    - Color Bass
    - Electronica
    - Ambient (Partial)
    - Orchestral (Partial)
    - Electronica (Partial)
- Keywords List:
    - Experimental Bass Design
    - Melancholy
    - Glitch
    - Dramatic
    - Ambient Pads
    - Piano
    - Ethereal & “Crystaline” Elements/Instruments
    - Orchestral Elements
    - Noise Elements
- Below is a list of things which might do a disservice to your submission:
    - Even if the genre is more or less correct and uplifting/progressive, we want to avoid something which feels too “happy”, but more “dramatic” and “cinematic”.
    - We are not exactly looking for classic “otogecore” types of music, or speedcore.
    - Do not concern yourself with BPM, and focus on picking a BPM which does your arrangement justice.
    - Breakcore elements must be cleared of copyright.
    - Trance, Hypertrance and general Drum and Bass might not be perfectly accurate for the compilation, but depending on the match of atmosphere, they can be permitted. We are not looking for “Y2K” elements, or something that people might refer to as “atmospheric jungle” or liquid drum and bass.
    - Think of artists such as Taishi, 猫叉Master, An, Feryquitous, 黒皇帝, ak+q, Supire…
- Due to the nature of the album, there might be some overlap with album concepts like hollow horizons, Escapism, TREMOR or even UPRISING. If it helps to understand the theme of HOWLING better, you can take inspiration from our Touhou album Border in Stasis.
- Although there might be an overlap in your song compared to a different album series, it is always worth checking in with the supervisor and asking if it could be a good fit to the compilation.
</WrappableParagraph>
